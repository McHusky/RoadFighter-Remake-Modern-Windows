## Modern Windows patch

This repository adds modern display handling, borderless presentation, Alt+Tab fixes, flicker-free backbuffering, audio-buffer improvements and runtime volume control through a direct patch of the original Windows executable.

The intent is preservation and compatibility: the original gameplay, art, maps, music and game logic remain the work of their original authors.
